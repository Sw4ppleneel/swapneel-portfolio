PRJ—01 IN PRODUCTION SaaS

Tulips.edu

school ERP · 4 paying institutional clients

Multi-tenant school ERP: student records, fees (UPI deep links + QR), academics, and parent communication across three surfaces — public site, staff ERP, parent portal — on one SPA. Tenant isolation is enforced in middleware with tenant_id on every table; every API route is role-checked across five roles; every state change emits an immutable audit event.

Failure-mode thinkingThe most frequent failure in an Indian school isn't the server — it's the teacher's connectivity. Attendance is offline-first: writes queue in IndexedDB and background-sync on recovery. File uploads go direct to R2 via presigned URLs, so storage failures never touch the app server. Migrations are raw versioned SQL, because schemas holding fee records don't get ORM surprises.

Research

Causal inference on real-world health data

Continuity of Care on Digital Health Platforms — research assistantship with Prof. Anuj Kapoor (University of Missouri), on a large-scale digital physiotherapy dataset.

When a physiotherapist exits unexpectedly, it breaks a patient's care mid-episode. These provider-side shocks are plausibly exogenous — clean variation for asking how much continuity causally matters, and for whom. The hard part: the treatment is a path through an adaptive system, where standard regression breaks down.

My work spans the identification strategy and the estimation pipeline — including pressure-testing parallel-trends assumptions across two difference-in-differences designs and validating dose-response gradients.

Identification

Staggered DiD (Callaway–Sant'Anna), provider exits as instruments, discrete-time hazard models.

Causal ML

Double/debiased ML, causal forests, R-/DR-learners for heterogeneous treatment effects across patient fragility dimensions.

Sequence models

Transformer encoders over care trajectories, counterfactual RNNs, representation balancing (TARNet/CFRNet).
